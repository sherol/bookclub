# Design

- `logo.svg` — the Bookclub squirrel logo: a squirrel drawn from the letters b·o·o·k·c·l·u·b, perched on an open book.
- `mock/` — the app mock: Portfolio board (desktop), Today and Club (phone), and the logo, in the dark navy and orange scheme taken from the logo.
  The `.dc.html` files are the source of the [Bookclub app mock](https://claude.ai/artifact/DThARwmnjmpAadq5AWzgEB) design canvas and render there, not as standalone pages. `canvas.json` holds the artboard layout.

Book titles, member names and numbers in the mock are placeholders in [brackets].
